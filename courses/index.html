Basic Level

What will I learn in the Basic Technical Analysis course?

We cover technical analysis step by step, including price behaviour, market psychology and candlestick patterns.

Is this course suitable for beginners?

Yes. It introduces core technical analysis concepts and helps learners build a foundation in chart reading and market analysis.

Medium Level

How can this course help improve my trading decisions?

It explores advanced strategies, market behaviour and structured decision-making techniques to help you develop a more systematic approach to analysing opportunities. It does not promise specific trading results.

What will I learn at the Medium Level?

Advanced candlestick patterns, futures and options concepts, market trends and positional trading strategies.

Advanced Level

Who should join the Advanced Technical Analysis course?

It is intended for experienced traders who want to study advanced market behaviour, complex setups and analytical skills.

Will I learn to analyse market trends?

Yes. The syllabus includes trend analysis and potential price movements across equities and F&O. Confirm any additional segment coverage with the course provider.

What makes this course different?

It focuses on in-depth technical analysis, chart study, advanced trading concepts and a structured, disciplined approach to learning.

Will the course include practical learning?

The content includes chart analysis, candlestick studies and trading strategy concepts. Confirm availability of live sessions, individual mentoring and practical assignments with the course provider before enrolment.
