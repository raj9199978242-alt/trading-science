Stock Market Course

Three clear levels, taught one-to-one.

Learn what you need at your own pace through personalised live sessions. Start with the basics, move into technical analysis, then build a responsible trading system.

01

Beginner - Build Your Foundation

Why learn this: You need a clear base before reading charts, placing orders, or discussing strategies.

Application: Understand market language, basic instruments, chart reading, and risk before entering deeper topics.

  • Financial and stock markets
  • Stocks, ETFs and other instruments
  • NSE, BSE and market participants
  • Orders, brokers and trading workflow
  • Market terminology
  • Reading charts and candlesticks
  • Risk management basics
  • Trading psychology
02

Intermediate - Learn Technical Analysis

Why learn this: Technical analysis helps you understand market structure instead of reacting randomly to price movement.

Application: Analyse trends, levels, patterns, indicators, entries, exits, and stop-loss planning with structure.

  • Market structure and trends
  • Support and resistance
  • Candlestick and chart patterns
  • Volume analysis
  • Moving averages
  • RSI, MACD, VWAP and indicators
  • Fibonacci analysis
  • Breakouts and pullbacks
  • Multi-timeframe analysis
  • Entry, exit and stop-loss techniques
  • Backtesting and trading journals
03

Advanced - Build Your Trading System

Why learn this: Advanced learning connects price action, risk, psychology, and review into a repeatable process.

Application: Create a personal trading plan, manage positions responsibly, study live cases, and review performance.

  • Advanced market structure
  • Price action and supply demand
  • Liquidity and breakout analysis
  • Advanced volume and VWAP
  • Options and Open Interest
  • Advanced trade management
  • Position sizing and risk management
  • Strategy development
  • Backtesting and performance analysis
  • Live market case studies
  • Personal trading plan
