About Trading Science

Practical stock market learning with Kundan Kumar.

Trading Science helps learners understand the market through structured, personalised, one-to-one Zoom sessions.

View CoursesBook Session

Founder and CEO

Kundan Kumar

Mr. Kundan Kumar is an MBA in Finance with more than 16 years of stock market experience and brokerage-industry exposure since 2008.

His teaching focuses on technical analysis, market behaviour, trading psychology, risk awareness, and practical chart discussion. The goal is to help learners build skill and discipline, not to promise fixed outcomes.

Education First

No tips, calls, investment advice, or guaranteed profit claims.

Personalised

Sessions are based on the learner’s level and exact questions.

Flexible

Book topic-wise hourly sessions instead of a long fixed package.

Online

Learn live through Zoom from anywhere in India.

A Clear Learning Standard

Understand the process, not just the terminology.

Market education is most useful when it explains how to think through a question, what information is missing, and where uncertainty remains.

Structured progression

Start with market basics, then build chart-reading skills and explore more advanced topics when the foundations are clear.

Practical discussion

Use examples and learner questions to connect concepts with charts, market context, and a written review process.

Honest boundaries

Trading Science offers education, not investment advice, trade calls, account handling, or a promise of financial results.

Independent reading can complement lessons. Zerodha Varsity offers free learning modules, and SEBI Investor provides investor education and risk information.
