Founder's Journey

Mr. Kundan Kumar is an MBA in Finance with more than 16 years of experience in the stock market. He has worked in managerial roles within the brokerage industry since 2008, developing extensive experience in technical analysis, market behaviour, and trading psychology.

Through Trading Science, he aims to share this practical experience with learners and help them develop the knowledge, confidence, and discipline required to approach the financial markets responsibly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start learning with Trading Science?

Share what you want to learn or where you feel stuck. Our consultant will guide you toward the right topic and help you book a one-to-one Zoom session.

Do I need to buy a fixed course package?

No. You can book focused hourly sessions based on the topic you want to understand. The cost depends on the subject and time required.

Who teaches the one-to-one sessions?

Sessions are designed around guidance from Mr. Kundan Kumar, Founder and CEO of Trading Science, with market experience since 2008.

Will you provide trading tips or profit guarantees?

No. Trading Science is an education platform. The focus is learning, analysis, risk awareness, and disciplined decision-making, not guaranteed outcomes.
