Founder’s Journey

Practical market learning, explained simply

Mr. Kundan Kumar is an MBA in Finance with more than 16 years of experience in the stock market. He has worked in managerial roles within the brokerage industry since 2008, building practical experience in technical analysis, market behaviour, and trading psychology.

Through Trading Science, he helps learners develop knowledge, confidence, and discipline. The focus is education and decision quality, not tips or unrealistic promises.

How he approaches teaching

Sessions begin with the learner’s current understanding and questions. Concepts are broken into manageable steps, discussed with chart examples, and connected to risk and decision-making. Learners are encouraged to ask why an idea may or may not apply, rather than memorise a prediction.

The goal is to leave with clearer concepts and a repeatable way to study markets independently. Learning outcomes vary by individual; market participation always involves risk.

Teaching Principles

Education built around clarity and responsibility

A trustworthy learning experience should make its scope clear and leave room for questions.

Start with context

Understand the instrument, market conditions, and terminology before discussing chart setups.

Include risk in the lesson

Discuss uncertainty, position sizing, and the limits of any analytical method alongside the concept.

Keep the learner in control

Sessions are educational. They do not provide personalised investment recommendations, calls, or profit guarantees.

Trading Science provides market education only. Examples are for learning and are not recommendations to buy or sell securities.
